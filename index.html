About Us

Digital Governance for Rural Development

Ahmednagar Connect is a centralized web-based portal for Ahmednagar district to monitor the planning, execution, financial utilization, verification, and completion of rural infrastructure works — including village roads, school buildings, water supply, drainage, and public facilities — implemented through district, taluka, and village-level authorities. The portal replaces scattered paper records with a transparent digital workflow.

Our Vision

To provide real-time visibility of rural infrastructure projects across the district, improve accountability at every administrative level, and enable faster, evidence-based decisions.

What We Do

Centralize project, financial, and progress data. Enable monitoring from village to district level. Improve transparency through date-stamped, geo-tagged evidence.

Our Commitment

Reduce paperwork, duplicate entries, and reporting delays — ensuring every rupee and every work is accounted for, visibly and transparently.

Village / Gram Panchayat

Engineer / Verifier

Taluka Officer
Monitors taluka-level progress, delays, issues, and fund utilization
District / CEO Authority
Oversees district-wide supervision, approvals, dashboards, and escalations
